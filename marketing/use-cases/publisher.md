# Use Case: Publisher Mode

**For:** Niche bloggers, content sites, and independent publishers who display ads and track affiliate links.

**One-sentence promise:** Put your ads where they actually get clicked, track every affiliate link, and stop juggling plugins — with one free plugin.

## What you get

- **Smart ad placements** — place ads after paragraph 3, in sticky footer bars, or as exit-intent popups. Anywhere except the sidebar black hole where banner blindness lives.
- **Affiliate link tracking** — cloak ugly URLs into clean `yoursite.com/go/product-name` links. Every click is logged. Broken links get flagged before your readers hit a 404.
- **AdSense without the headache** — enter your Publisher ID once. Create AdSense ad units with the same targeting options as every other ad type: by device, user role, category, and schedule.

You run a niche blog — recipes, tech reviews, personal finance, fitness — and you want to earn from display ads and affiliate recommendations without your site turning into a banner farm. You care about the reader experience and you want to know which content actually drives revenue.
