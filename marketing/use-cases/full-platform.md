# Use Case: Full Platform Mode

**For:** Sites that run everything at once — display ads, affiliate links, a classifieds marketplace, and a self-service advertiser portal — and want one coherent system instead of four plugins.

**One-sentence promise:** One plugin, one dashboard, one place where all your monetization data lives — from affiliate link clicks to advertiser campaign spend to classified listing revenue.

---

![Advertiser Portal — overview showing all active revenue streams](../../docs/website/images/pro/portal-overview.png)

---

## What you get

- **All monetization in one database** — display ads, affiliate link clicks, classified listing fees, and campaign spend all appear in the same analytics dashboard. You can compare revenue sources and spot what's growing without bouncing between tools.
- **A/B testing across ad creatives** — run split tests on banners, in-content ads, and portal submissions. Pro tracks statistical significance and marks a winner automatically once enough impressions accumulate.
- **Community platform support** — if you run BuddyPress or BuddyBoss, advertiser profiles appear on member profile pages automatically. Classifieds sellers get public seller pages at `/seller/{slug}/`.

---

## This is for you if…

Your site already does several things — a blog, a community, maybe some affiliate content — and you keep adding plugins to cover each revenue stream. You want to consolidate, get unified reporting, and have room to grow into classifieds or an advertiser portal without rebuilding anything.

The free version covers display ads and affiliate links. Upgrading to Pro adds the classifieds marketplace, advertiser portal, campaign management, and advanced analytics on top of everything you already have.
